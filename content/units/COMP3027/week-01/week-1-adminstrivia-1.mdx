## Summary

Week 1 administrative briefing for COMP3027 Algorithm Design: logistics rather than algorithmic content, but sets the contract for how the unit is assessed.

- **Assessment split:** 4 written assignments (30%, equally weighted, each with a mandatory short-answer part plus an optional in-depth problem mirroring the final exam), 12 weekly quizzes (best 10 of 12, 10%), and a supervised in-person exam (60%). School policy requires at least 40% on the exam to pass.
- **Exam guarantee:** one question very close to an optional in-depth problem from the assignments will appear on the final (10/60 marks).
- **Late policy:** automatic 5-day simple extension on every assignment; hard close 5 days after due date.
- **Reading:** Kleinberg-Tardos, Erickson, Roughgarden — none compulsory.
- **AI policy:** generative AI banned in supervised exams unless explicitly permitted; declare any AI use otherwise.

**Exam focus:** no algorithmic content tested here. Use this deck to confirm submission deadlines, the 40% exam hurdle, and the fact that revising the optional in-depth assignment problems is the highest-leverage exam prep.
