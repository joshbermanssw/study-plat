## Summary

Lecture 3 introduces Divide-and-Conquer: split into independent subproblems, delegate to the "recursion fairy", combine. Correctness by induction on n; running time by recurrence T(n) = aT(n/b) + f(n) solved via Master theorem, recursion-tree unrolling, or substitution.

- **Binary search:** T(n) = T(n/2) + O(1) ⇒ O(log n).
- **MergeSort:** T(n) = 2T(n/2) + O(n) ⇒ O(n log n); merge step is linear via two-pointer scan.
- **Maximum-sum contiguous subarray:** three cases — left half, right half, or spanning the midpoint (linear left-suffix + right-prefix scan). T(n) = 2T(n/2) + O(n) ⇒ O(n log n).
- **Counting inversions:** strengthen the recursion fairy to also return the sorted list; merge-and-count tallies cross inversions in O(n) ⇒ O(n log n) total.
- **Closest pair of points:** divide by vertical line, recurse on halves; in the 2δ-strip sort by y and check next 7 neighbours (geometric packing argument). O(n log n) with pre-sorted lists.

**Exam focus:** state and solve the recurrence (Master theorem cases, recursion tree); design the combine step and prove correctness by induction. Recurrence-solving is explicitly flagged for the quiz.
