## Summary

Week 9 lecture, the centrepiece of Module 3: the **Value Proposition Canvas** and the **Business Model Canvas** as design tools for innovation management.

- **Value Proposition Canvas** (Osterwalder): **Customer Profile** (jobs, pains, gains) on one side; **Value Map** (products/services, pain relievers, gain creators) on the other. **Fit** occurs when the map alleviates extreme pains and creates essential gains.
- **Customer Jobs**: functional, social, personal/emotional. **Gains** classified as required, expected, desired, unexpected. **Pains** include problems, obstacles, risks.
- **Value Proposition Pyramid**: features → benefits → experience — layered claims of value.
- **Business Model Canvas (9 blocks)**: customer segments, value propositions, channels, customer relationships, revenue streams, key resources, key activities, key partnerships, cost structure.
- **Iterate**: VPC and BMC are hypothesis tools — test, learn, pivot. Pair with Customer Development and Lean Startup loops from Week 8.

**Exam focus**: high-probability applied question — given a startup scenario, populate a VPC or BMC; explain "fit" and justify pain relievers vs gain creators. Markers want coherence between customer pains and product features, not just filled boxes.
