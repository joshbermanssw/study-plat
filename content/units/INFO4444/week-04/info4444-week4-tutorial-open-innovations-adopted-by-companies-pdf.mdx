## Summary

Week 4 tutorial applying Chesbrough's Open Innovation framework: groups analyse how a real organisation operationalises openness through platforms, APIs, competitions, or open data.

- **Case options**: **Google Chrome Extensions** (platform + web API enabling third-party innovation), **Microsoft Imagine Cup** (global innovation competition), **Tech Council of Australia – Parliamentary Innovation Showcase 2025** (crowdsourcing + open data).
- **API/platform mechanics**: lower the cost of complementary innovation, capture data and revenue, and create ecosystem lock-in.
- **Strategic goal**: speed time-to-market, externalise risk, tap "Joy's Law" talent outside the firm.
- **Mutual benefits**: company gets diverse ideas, brand reach, and pipeline; users/developers get distribution, tools, and recognition.
- **External platform vs own ecosystem**: use external when the market is unfamiliar or non-core; build your own when you can capture network effects and platform rents.

**Exam focus**: short essay applying the three modes of open innovation (outside-in, inside-out, coupled) to a named case, plus a question on when to use external platforms vs internal ecosystems — markers want explicit trade-offs.
