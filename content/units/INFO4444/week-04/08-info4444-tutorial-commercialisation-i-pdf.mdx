## Summary

Week 8 tutorial (in the week-4 folder) opening Module 3 on Commercialisation: how startups operate, the Customer Development Process, and Agile/Lean in early-stage ventures.

- **Startup vs established company**: startups search for a repeatable business model under high uncertainty; incumbents execute a known one. Structure, risk tolerance, and customer base differ.
- **Customer Development (Steve Blank)**: customer discovery → validation → creation → company building. Contrast with the linear New Product Introduction model (slide 21) used by incumbents.
- **Product-market fit**: the moment your value proposition matches a paying segment's pain; tested via problem and solution hypotheses.
- **Agile / Lean (Eric Ries)**: build–measure–learn loop using MVPs, iterative releases, real user feedback. Agile Manifesto principles apply.
- **Pairs to analyse**: Canva vs Adobe, Zoom vs Around/Butter, Up vs Big 4 banks, TikTok vs Netflix.

**Exam focus**: short essay contrasting Customer Development with traditional New Product Introduction, plus an applied question on whether an incumbent can credibly adopt Agile — markers want the structural-incentive argument, not just buzzwords.
