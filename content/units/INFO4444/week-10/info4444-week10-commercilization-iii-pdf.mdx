## Summary

Week 10 lecture: **Capital and Fundraising for IT Innovation**, closing Module 3 by connecting the BMC's revenue/cost blocks to real funding markets.

- **Why raise capital**: customer discovery → MVP → customer validation → scale demand → build channels each demand cash before revenue.
- **Valley of Death / J-curve**: cumulative losses before product-market fit; hardware (e.g. Rethink Robotics, $150M and gone) is especially brutal.
- **Funding stages and investors**: bootstrapping/FFF → angels → seed VC → Series A/B/C → growth → exit (IPO, M&A). 2025–26 AI mega-rounds: OpenAI $122B, Anthropic $30B, xAI $20B. Australian seed benchmarks ~$1.6–1.9M.
- **Revenue Streams (BMC block 5)**: asset sale, usage fee, subscription, lending/renting, licensing, brokerage, advertising. Quantify value, capture a slice.
- **Cost Structure**: fixed vs variable, economies of scale/scope; cost-driven vs value-driven business models.

**Exam focus**: short answer defining the Valley of Death and matching funding type to stage, plus an applied question on revenue stream selection for a given startup (e.g. SaaS vs marketplace). Markers expect numbers and stage-fit reasoning.
