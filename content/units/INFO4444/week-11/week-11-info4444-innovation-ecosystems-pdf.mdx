## Summary

Week 11 lecture opening Module 4 (Innovation at Scale): how **innovation systems** and **innovation ecosystems** support innovation beyond the firm.

- **Innovation system**: an open network of organisations interacting under framework conditions to produce and diffuse innovations of economic, social, or environmental value. Australian players: domestic business environment, international trade, human capital, collaboration, R&D investment.
- **System outcomes**: new tech/knowledge creation, adoption of existing tech, productivity growth (the main economic channel).
- **Innovation journey phases**: innovator development → startup (BMC, MVP) → scale-up → growth → exit (M&A, IPO); startup count drops at each phase.
- **Triple Helix Model**: University (knowledge), Government (policy and funding), Industry (application) interact to drive innovation.
- **Global benchmarks**: **Singapore** — government-as-founder, deep-tech hub, EDB+SGInnovate, NUS/NTU alignment; **Silicon Valley** — densest VC capital, Stanford/Berkeley pipeline, risk culture, AI/space/crypto leadership.

**Exam focus**: short answer defining innovation system vs ecosystem and the Triple Helix, plus an essay comparing two ecosystems (e.g. Singapore vs Silicon Valley) and recommending what Australia should adopt — markers want named institutions and mechanisms.
