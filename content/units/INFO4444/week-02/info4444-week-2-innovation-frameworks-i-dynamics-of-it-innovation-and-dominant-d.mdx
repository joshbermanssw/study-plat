## Summary

Week 2 lecture introducing the foundational frameworks for Module 1: how innovations spread through populations and how product categories converge on a dominant design.

- **Diffusion of Innovation (Rogers)**: innovation communicated through channels over time within a social system; five adopter categories (innovators, early adopters, early majority, late majority, laggards) form the S-curve.
- **Rate-of-adoption attributes**: relative advantage, compatibility, simplicity, trial-ability, observe-ability — plus change-agent effort. QWERTY vs Dvorak shows superior tech can still lose.
- **Crossing the Chasm (Moore)**: the gap between early adopters and the early majority is the make-or-break point for mainstream dominance.
- **Gartner Hype Cycle**: trigger → peak of inflated expectations → trough of disillusionment → slope of enlightenment → plateau.
- **Dominant Design (Utterback-Abernathy)**: fluid → transitional → specific phase; first mover vs second mover vs **fast second** strategies.

**Exam focus**: short-answer defining the five adopters and the chasm, plus an essay applying diffusion attributes or dominant-design phases to a named technology (e.g. smart speakers, EVs).
