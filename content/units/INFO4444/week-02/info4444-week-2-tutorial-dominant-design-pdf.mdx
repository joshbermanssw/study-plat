## Summary

Week 2 tutorial applying the Module 1 dominant-design framework: groups pick an emerging IT innovation expected to matter in the next five years and argue whether a dominant design has yet emerged.

- **Task**: choose one impactful IT innovation (current or near-future); justify its significance.
- **Define the product category** clearly before you can claim a dominant design exists — categories shape the comparison set.
- **Compare design differences** across competing products in the chosen category to test for convergence.
- **Dominant design test**: look for shared architecture, interfaces, and user expectations across competitors.
- **Defend under challenge**: each group fields ~10 minutes of counterpoints, so your reasoning must survive scrutiny.

**Exam focus**: expect a short essay asking you to nominate an emerging IT technology, define its product category, and argue whether a dominant design exists — markers reward precise category definition and explicit comparison criteria, not just listing examples.
