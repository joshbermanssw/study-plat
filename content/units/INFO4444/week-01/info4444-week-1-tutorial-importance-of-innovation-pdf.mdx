## Summary

Week 1 tutorial setting up the unit's core question: why and how do governments support IT innovation, framed through the lens of pitching an Australian IT startup for public funding.

- **Government motivations**: correct market failures, build national capability, secure economic and strategic advantage in IT-enabled sectors.
- **Government roles**: funder (grants), regulator, customer (procurement), infrastructure provider, and convenor of research/industry.
- **Why publicly fund foundational IT** (internet, GPS, AI, biotech): high risk, long horizons, spillover benefits, and dual-use strategic value private capital won't bear alone.
- **Australia's Science and Research Priorities**: align startup pitches to these to maximise funding chances.
- **Innovation and Science Australia Annual Report 2021-22** case studies illustrate how specific programs benefit industries.

**Exam focus**: expect a short-answer or essay question asking you to justify government intervention in IT innovation and map a hypothetical startup to Australia's stated priorities, citing a named program as evidence.
