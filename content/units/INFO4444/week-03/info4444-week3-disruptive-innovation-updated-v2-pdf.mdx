## Summary

Week 3 lecture on Christensen's Disruptive Innovation and the Innovator's Dilemma, closing out Module 1's innovation frameworks.

- **Four innovation models**: incremental (improve features), radical (new tech), sustaining (new business model, same tech), disruptive (both new tech and new business model).
- **Disruptive vs sustaining (Christensen)**: disruptors target overlooked segments; sustainers move upmarket. Smaller firms beat incumbents who over-serve high-end customers.
- **Two disruption types**: **low-end disruption** (cheaper "good enough" product attacks under-served segments, e.g. mini-mills, Netflix DVD) and **new-market disruption** (creates non-consumers into consumers, e.g. personal computers).
- **Five phases**: recognise industry gap → launch novel product → build momentum → disrupt market → establish new standard.
- **Innovator's Dilemma**: rational incumbents focus on best customers, ignore low-margin entrants, then get displaced. Value network and transformative tech determine success.

**Exam focus**: classic essay — "explain Christensen's disruptive innovation theory and apply it to [Netflix / Uber / Coursera]." Markers want the low-end vs new-market distinction and a clear value-network argument.
