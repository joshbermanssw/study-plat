## Summary

Week 7 tutorial (delivered in the week-3 folder) on distributed innovation, using Apple's App Store and Google's Play Store as the worked case for platform ecosystems, user innovation, and IP protection.

- **Platform ecosystem roles**: platform owner (Apple/Google), complementors/developers, end users, third-party services — each contributes value (innovation, revenue, data, reach).
- **Why platforms exist**: owners capture 15–30% revenue cuts, lock in users, harvest data, and outsource innovation to thousands of developers.
- **IP protection**: patents (technical), trademarks (brand), trade secrets (algorithms), plus curation/approval gates that police cloning and misuse.
- **Ecosystem acquisitions**: Workflow→Shortcuts, Shazam, Waze, Where 2, Dark Sky — big tech buys rather than builds when startups prove product-market fit.
- **User innovation drivers**: low-code tools, open APIs, accessible AI lower the cost of becoming an innovator.

**Exam focus**: short-answer on platform-ecosystem roles and IP types, plus an essay asking why incumbents acquire rather than build, with reference to a specific case.
