## Summary

Week 11 tutorial (in the week-5 folder) opening Module 4 (Innovation at Scale), using the **Sydney Startup Hub** as the local lens and comparing it to Silicon Valley and Singapore.

- **Valley of Death**: the funding gap between proof-of-concept and commercial revenue where many startups die; angels and early VCs fill it.
- **Investor types by stage**: angels and accelerators at seed; VCs at Series A onward; strategic/corporate VCs and PE at scale-up and growth; IPO/acquirers at exit.
- **Innovation system vs ecosystem**: a *system* is the formal institutional setup (policy, universities, funding bodies); an *ecosystem* is the broader network of actors, culture, and informal links — e.g. Silicon Valley vs Australia's National Innovation System.
- **Enablers**: talent pipelines (universities), capital, mentorship, government programs, regulatory support, and co-location.
- **Australian context**: universities (e.g. Sydney, UNSW) drive commercialisation; learn from Singapore's state-led model or Silicon Valley's VC density.

**Exam focus**: short answer defining Valley of Death and the system-vs-ecosystem distinction, plus an essay arguing what Australia should adopt from another ecosystem and why.
