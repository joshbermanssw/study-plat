## Summary

This Week 4 tutorial inspects HTTP request/response messages and browser fetching behaviour, reinforcing the protocol and rendering material from the lecture on the critical rendering path.

- Crafting raw HTTP/1.0 and HTTP/1.1 requests over `telnet` (note the mandatory `Host:` header in 1.1 and persistent connections vs immediate close in 1.0).
- Reading status line (protocol version, status code, status message) and headers (`Server`, `ETag`, `Content-Type`).
- Conditional GETs with `If-None-Match` returning `304 Not Modified` with empty body.
- DevTools Network panel: per-request timing, status codes, identifying which server sends cache directives.
- Rendering events: `DOMContentLoaded` vs `Load`, and why image/asset requests delay the latter.

Exam focus: given a raw request/response, identify method, version, status, and caching headers; explain the gap between request completion and `DOMContentLoaded`, and why a `.webp` request takes far longer than the HTML.
